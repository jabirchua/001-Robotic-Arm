# Mechanical Requirements — Draft 01

## 1. Project Purpose
Design a desktop robotic arm for electronics work, object positioning, and equipment manipulation.

## 2. Payload Requirements
- Target payload: 200–500 g
- Design priority: Hold 500 g at a reach of 25–30 cm
- Example objects: Magnifying lens, PCB, small equipment

## 3. Mechanical Configuration
- Arm type: Articulated robotic arm (proposed)
- Degrees of freedom: To be determined
- Shoulder-to-end-effector reach: 250–300 mm target
- Link lengths: To be determined
- Joint bearing arrangement: To be determined

## 4. Performance Requirements
- Stable holding of payload
- Precise and repeatable positioning
- Minimize deflection and joint backlash
